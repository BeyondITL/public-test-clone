Claude drafts in your own account. This page checks every line against your transcript, lets you change the words and the look, and exports slides you can post. It does not generate text and uploads nothing.

How it works
  1. Press Make your own. Add your brand details and paste a transcript you have the rights to. SRT and VTT files keep their timecodes.
  2. Press Copy request for Claude, paste it into Claude with one of the kit's skills installed, then paste Claude's reply back here.
  3. Read the draft against your source, change the words or the look beside the carousel, approve, and export or copy.

What it cannot do: it does not watch video, clip video, post for you, or judge whether a paraphrase is true. That review is yours.
