You need your own Claude account for drafting. The kit checks where each line came from. Whether the wording is right is your call, and the workbench makes you confirm it before export.

How it works

  1. Prepare the lesson

    Paste the captions of a recorded lesson, or your lesson notes as plain text. Each passage gets an ID. Plain text has no timecodes, and none are invented.

  2. Draft with the lesson skill

    The lesson to carousel skill is in the free edition. Install it in your own Claude account and paste the request the workbench writes. Claude returns the draft as structured data.

  3. Check against what you taught

    Each slide shows which passage it came from. Fix anything that drifts from the lesson, approve, then download PNG slides or a PDF, or copy the scripts and posts.

Each draft contains

  • A carousel of 5 to 10 slides
  • 1 to 8 reel scripts, each with the source time range it draws on
  • 2 to 12 posts, labelled by platform
  • Review notes listing what the draft was unsure about

What it does not do

  • It does not watch your video. It works from the transcript. Anything about what is on screen needs your own check.
  • It does not make video. Reel scripts are scripts. There is no clipping or MP4 export.
  • It does not post for you, and it does not download or scrape anything from the web.
  • It does not prove a claim is true. It proves each line cites a real passage. Reading it is still your job.
  • It does not include AI credits. Drafting happens in your Claude account, under Anthropic's terms for that account.

Questions

Is the lesson skill really free?
Yes. The free edition has the lesson to carousel skill, one worked example and the full workbench. It does not expire. Claude usage is not included.
Can I use my notes instead of a recording?
Yes. Plain text works. Each paragraph becomes a passage with an ID. Reels then have no source times, and the workbench says so.
Which Claude plan do I need?
Anthropic's help centre lists skills as available on Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans, with code execution switched on (read 11 November 2026). The kit does not include a Claude subscription.
Where do my files go?
The workbench reads them in your browser and sends them nowhere. When you paste the source into Claude, Anthropic processes it under the terms of your own account.
Will it check that my slides are correct?
It checks that each slide cites a passage that exists in your lesson. It cannot tell whether a paraphrase is accurate, so you read each one before you approve.
Is this made or endorsed by Anthropic?
No. It is an independent product that works with Claude.
