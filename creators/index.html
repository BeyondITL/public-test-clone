How it works

  1. Bring your transcript

    Use the SRT or VTT captions of your video, or paste a plain transcript. Each passage gets an ID. Your timecodes are kept as they are, and none are made up.

  2. Draft in your Claude

    Install a skill in your own Claude account and paste the request the workbench writes. The lesson skill is free. The webinar and product demo skills come with the complete edition, which is not on sale yet.

  3. Check, edit, copy

    Read each slide, script and post beside the line it cites and fix the wording in plain fields. Approve, then download the slides or copy each script and post.

What it does not do

  • It does not watch your video. It works from the transcript. Anything about what is on screen needs your own check.
  • It does not make video. Reel scripts are scripts. There is no clipping or MP4 export.
  • It does not post for you, and it does not download or scrape anything from the web.
  • It does not prove a claim is true. It proves each line cites a real passage. Reading it is still your job.
  • It does not include AI credits. Drafting happens in your Claude account, under Anthropic's terms for that account.

Questions

Does it work with my videos?
It works with transcripts, not video files. Any recording with SRT or VTT captions, or a plain transcript, can be used. It does not look at the picture, so check anything about what is on screen yourself.
Will it post to my accounts?
No. It does not connect to any platform. You download the slides and copy the text, then post them yourself.
Can it make my reels?
It writes reel scripts and shows the source time each one draws on, so you can find the moment in your footage. It does not cut or export video.
Where do my files go?
The workbench reads them in your browser and sends them nowhere. When you paste the source into Claude, Anthropic processes it under the terms of your own account.
Do I have to deal with code or data?
No. You paste Claude's whole reply and press Check the reply. Slides, reel scripts and posts are edited in ordinary fields beside the carousel, and each script and post has its own Copy button once you approve.
Is this made or endorsed by Anthropic?
No. It is an independent product that works with Claude.
